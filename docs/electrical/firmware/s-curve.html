Project source: Robotics-Arm ↗

The whole point here was to make a S-Curve generator for motors, to allow little vibrations, and an accurate position control scheme, to be fed to the ESCs which is to run on top of the PID that the ESCs use in tandem with FOC.

Overview - Trajectory Control

The goal of trajectory control for this part of hte project is to break down a desired position (the setpoint) into a series of intermediate positions. These intermediate positions will guide the motor in a controlled fashion to the setpoint

What we want:

  • Be able to change setpoints on the fly
  • A smooth transition to the setpoint
  • Control with precise position
  • Have different parameters we can tune to fit each joint

Overview - S Curve

  • S-Curve motion offers a smooth acceleration profile for a motor to reach the desired target
  • It is the result of taking a specific applied Jerk profile, and integrating back to achieve position

Why is this good

  • Very smooth control over the arm
  • Reduces electrical stress on the motor
  • Reduces mechanical vibrations
  • Prevents any discontinuities

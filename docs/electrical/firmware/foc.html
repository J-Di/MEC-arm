Planned

Field oriented control

FOC is the proposed motor-control method for all seven brushless actuators.

Goal

The control strategy is intended to provide smooth torque production and low speed behavior at the joints. The implementation and tuning are not documented yet.

Future content

Record coordinate transforms, current-loop design, sampling and PWM timing, encoder alignment, tuning results, and fault handling after firmware bring-up.

This project is ongoing. Design goals, planned work, and measured results will be distinguished as the pages grow.

Joint position control

A joint-level position loop will use encoder feedback to command repeatable motion.

Requirements

The ESC goals call for smooth low speed position control with magnetic encoder feedback. Joint speed targets and limits can be specified per axis as the mechanical design settles.

Future content

Add control-loop structure, trajectory interfaces, calibration procedure, joint limits, tuning plots, and measured tracking error.

This project is ongoing. Design goals, planned work, and measured results will be distinguished as the pages grow.

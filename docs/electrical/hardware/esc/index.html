Joint ESC

A compact brushless motor controller intended to live behind each joint motor and support smooth low speed position control.

Why mount it at the motor?

Locating the ESC at the motor simplifies joint wiring and lets the controller sense a magnetic encoder at the input shaft. One general controller design can then be reused across the arm's seven motors.

Current design goals

The board is being designed for field oriented control, magnetic position feedback, and protection against overcurrent, voltage faults, and excess temperature. Bring-up and testing are future milestones; performance is not yet characterized.

This project is ongoing. Design goals, planned work, and measured results will be distinguished as the pages grow.

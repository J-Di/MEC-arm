01 / THE PROJECT

Two disciplines.
One machine.

MEC stands for Mechanical Electrical Collaboration. It is the idea behind the arm and the way we’re building it.

Our goal is a functional, precise, practical robotic arm. We’re designing core mechanical and electrical components ourselves, then testing how they work together as a complete system.

The project is in development. Specifications and progress below reflect the current design, not a finished product.

02 / SYSTEM DESIGN

Inside the arm.

From motion at the joint to commands from the host.

01◎

Motion

Waist roll, shoulder pitch, elbow pitch, and a three axis roll–pitch–roll wrist. A motor driven gripper adds the end effector.

6 axes + gripper
02⌁

Actuation

Seven brushless motors are planned with custom cycloidal gearboxes. Current gearbox ratio targets include 19:1, 25:1, and 15:1.

Custom joint drives
03◇

Control

Field oriented control at each joint, on board magnetic encoder feedback, CAN communication, and a Raspberry Pi host.

Distributed control
04↯

Power

A 6S LiPo supply and a planned power distribution and coordination board will provide a central connection point for the system.

04 / ELECTRICAL HARDWARE

Control, close
to the motor.

Our custom ESC is intended to mount behind each motor. That places magnetic encoder feedback at the motor shaft and keeps joint wiring compact.

The board is being designed as a reusable controller across the arm’s joints, paired with a separate power distribution and coordination board.

ESC / DESIGN GOALSIN PROGRESS
